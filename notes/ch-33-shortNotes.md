Right now, only the person who created a company (the "owner") can use it. This chapter lets the owner invite other people to join their company — like inviting a teammate to help manage jobs.

# How inviting works, in plain words

The owner sends a request: "invite bob@email.com as a recruiter."
The server creates an invitation — basically a special one-time secret link.
The server emails that link to Bob.
Bob clicks the link, and (in a later chapter) that adds him to the company.
